# Third-party material

The MIT License in [LICENSE](LICENSE) covers the theme's own code, its build scripts and the ornaments those scripts draw. It does not cover the following material, which is included under its own terms.

- **Fonts embedded in `theme.css`:** EB Garamond, Cormorant Garamond, IM FELL English SC (outlines emboldened) and a CJK punctuation subset of Noto Serif SC, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. See [fonts/OFL.txt](fonts/OFL.txt).
- **Optional snippets in `snippets/`:** `sangreal-chinese-heading-font.css` contains a subset of 寒蝉活宋体 (ChillHuoSong) by ChillType, renamed "BA Heading Song" so that it does not use the Reserved Font Names; `sangreal-chinese-body-font.css` contains a subset of Noto Serif SC by Adobe. Both are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- **New-tab inscription:** rendered into an image with Manufacturing Consent, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- **Question callout icon:** the question mark of Playfair Display Bold (SIL Open Font License 1.1, © The Playfair Display Project Authors), combined with the chapter emblem.
- **Icons from [game-icons.net](https://game-icons.net):** by Lorc: bleeding-heart, book-aura, broken-skull, candle-light, chalice-drops, curly-wing, feather, holy-grail, hourglass, laurels, quill, ringing-bell, scroll-unfurled, sundial, two-feathers and winged-sword; by Delapouite: notebook and tower-flag. They are licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), and have been recoloured, cropped, re-centred or combined for this theme.
- **Wings on the properties frame:** traced from a public-domain engraving distributed by Heritage Library (HTC Heritage Type), used under that library's licence. That licence forbids redistributing the material itself, so the traced source files are not in this repository; the artwork is embedded only in the built `theme.css`.
- **Flourishes and ribbons:** modified from free illustrations by [frame-illust.com](https://frame-illust.com/), used under its terms of use. Those terms forbid redistributing the material itself, modified or not, so the source files are not in this repository; the artwork is embedded only in the built `theme.css`.
- **Failure callout icon:** modified from [ICOOON MONO](https://icooon-mono.com/), used under its terms of use. Those terms forbid redistributing the icon files themselves, so the source SVG is not in this repository; the icon is embedded only in the built `theme.css`.
- **Blood Angels emblem, Adeptus Mechanicus emblem and Imperial aquila:** trademarks of Games Workshop Ltd. The chapter emblem is redrawn for this theme; the other two come from the community "wh_icons" set. They appear in this unofficial fan work and are not licensed under the MIT License.
- **Paintings in `demo-vault/Assets`:** in the public domain. Fra Angelico, *The Annunciation* (c. 1426, Museo del Prado), and Camille Corot, *View of Florence from the Boboli Gardens* (c. 1835, Musée du Louvre), both via Wikimedia Commons.
