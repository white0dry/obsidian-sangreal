The gate at the edge of Hell, with no lock and no guard. Beyond it lie the vestibule and the Acheron, and on the far bank, [[Limbo]]. Dante reads its inscription at the opening of Canto III.

## The Inscription

The gate speaks in the first person: three lines on where it leads, three on who made it, three on what it asks. The last is the line everyone remembers: *All hope abandon, ye who enter in!*

## The Vestibule

Just inside run the souls who lived without disgrace and without praise, chasing a banner that never stops. Neither Heaven nor Hell will take them.
